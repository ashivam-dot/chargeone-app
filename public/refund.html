Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 20 October 2026

Cancelling Plus

Cancel anytime in Account → Cancel subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

  • If you were charged but your plan didn't activate, or you were charged twice, we refund the full amount.
  • If you bought a Trip Pass or your first month of Plus and haven't planned any long trip with it, you can ask for a full refund within 3 days of payment.
  • Otherwise, payments for a period already started are not refundable.

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the email you sign in with and the Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Delivery

ChargeOne is a digital service. Nothing is shipped. Your plan activates in the app as soon as the payment succeeds.
